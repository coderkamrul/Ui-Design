Page rating: `GET /docs/feedback?path=<this page's path>&helpful={true|false}&comment={comment}` records whether this page helped. The `comment` (URL-encoded, at most 500 characters) says what helped, or what was missing or wrong; a `helpful=false` rating needs it. It is anonymous and needs no key. Comments hold no code, secrets or personal data.
